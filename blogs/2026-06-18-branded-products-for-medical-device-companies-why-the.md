Many medical device companies still produce branded products, marketing materials, and trade show assets centrally before shipping them worldwide. While familiar, this model can create significant costs in freight, storage, customs, lead times, and waste. This article explores why local, on-demand production is becoming a more efficient alternative for global medical device organisations.
